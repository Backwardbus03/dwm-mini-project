About Q-CHAT-10

The Quantitative Checklist for Autism in Toddlers (Q-CHAT-10) is a validated screening tool for children aged 12-36 months. It helps identify early signs of autism spectrum disorder, enabling timely intervention and support.

⚠️

Important: This screening tool is for preliminary assessment only. It is not a diagnostic tool. Please consult a qualified developmental pediatrician or child psychologist for comprehensive evaluation.
