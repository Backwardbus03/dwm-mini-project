About This Data

This demographic data is sourced from a global autism screening study comprising {{ stats.total_participants }} participants across {{ stats.countries }} countries. The visualizations help identify patterns and trends in autism screening data across different populations and regions.

Note: This data is for research and educational purposes. Demographics are self-reported by participants during screening. There is missing data regarding country and ethnicity of the participant since these fields were optional and they chose to keep it confidential. Additionally, this data is different from the screener input data and should not be used for individual diagnosis.
